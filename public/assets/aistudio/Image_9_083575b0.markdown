## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-thinking, empathetic healthcare experience tailored for Gen Z patients and caregivers. The aesthetic bridges high-tech AI capability with human vulnerability, ditching sterile clinical tropes in favor of an uplifting, kinetic, and luminous environment.

- **Design Style:** Modern Neo-Glassmorphism infused with high-energy digital accents and organic softness. Translucent layered plates sit over dark or luminous backdrops, accentuated by precise micro-glows, tactile rounded elements, and airy typography.
- **Brand Personality:** Empathetic, frictionless, radiant, playful yet medically reliable.
- **Emotional Response:** Comfort without condescension, absolute clarity during medical stress, and modern delight when interacting with autonomous healthcare scheduling.

## Colors

The palette employs a deep, warm-tinted obsidian base (`#0F111A`) to reduce screen fatigue and make radiant bioluminescent accents pop with purpose:

- **Primary (`#8A4DFF` - Electric Violet):** Drives key calls to action, active navigation states, and primary NestAI agent accents.
- **Secondary (`#00F5D4` - Radiant Cyan / Neo-Mint):** Denotes vitality, completed confirmations, real-time availability slots, and positive metric indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#FF758F` - Gentle Coral Peach):** Used for caregiver alerts, medication logs, warm conversational prompts, and critical reminders.
- **Soft Lavender Tint (`#D8C5FF`):** Supplementary tint for secondary tags, subtle borders, and low-priority pill backgrounds.
- **Neutral Surface Foundations:**
  - Base Background: `#0F111A`
  - Elevated Container / Glass Tier 1: `rgba(28, 32, 48, 0.65)`
  - Active Surface / Glass Tier 2: `rgba(42, 48, 71, 0.75)`
  - High-Contrast Text: `#FFFFFF`
  - Subdued / Muted Text: `#9E9EB8`

## Typography

The typography centers on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to ensure geometric warmth, modern cadence, and high legibility at glanceable mobile distances. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Heavy weights (`700` and `800`) provide youthful visual weight, anchoring conversational AI prompts and doctor specialization headers.
- **Body:** Neutral weights (`400`) balanced with generous line heights (`1.5x`) guarantee stress-free comprehension of medical advice, clinical schedules, and dosage directions.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Crisp letter-spacing (`+0.02em`) on pill labels and timestamps to maintain crispness against glass backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

Mobile-first fluid architecture built on an 8pt base grid with a 4pt sub-grid for tight micro-alignments:

- **Canvas Margins:** Fixed `1.25rem` (20px) horizontal outer margin for thumb-reach edge clearance on handheld devices.
- **Grid Gutters:** `1rem` (16px) fluid separation for stacked schedule cards, provider grids, and multi-day horizontally scrollable selectors.
- **Component Padding Scale:**
  - Micro tags / status pills: `space-xs` (4px) vertical, `space-sm` (8px) horizontal.
  - Interactive inputs & quick chips: `space-sm` (8px) to `space-md` (16px).
  - Cards & modals: `space-lg` (24px) internal containment to afford comfortable breathing room for clinical telemetry.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages layered translucent plates (frosting) paired with luminous colored aura highlights rather than standard black shadows:

- **Level 1 (Subsurface Sheets):** `rgba(28, 32, 48, 0.45)` with `16px` backdrop-blur and a subtle `1px` inner rim of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Cards):** `rgba(35, 40, 60, 0.7)` with `24px` backdrop-blur, bounded by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Casts an ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Level 3 (NestAI Dynamic Glow):** AI chat interfaces and active booking slots emit an electric focal glow: `0 0 24px -2px rgba(138, 77, 255, 0.35)` to create soft focal guidance.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Drawers):** Dense glass base `rgba(18, 21, 31, 0.92)` with `32px` blur and an assertive top border gradient shifting from Electric Violet to Radiant Cyan.

## Shapes

The system implements continuous curvature with full pill primitives to communicate approachable care:

- **Pill Primitives:** Buttons, filter chips, prompt bubbles, and AI status indicators adopt continuous stadium/pill borders (`roundedness: 3` / `9999px`).
- **Cards & Bottom Sheets:** Standard cards leverage `1.5rem` (`24px`) radii; modal sheets utilize `2rem` (`32px`) top radii to feel organic, friendly, and soft under fingertip interaction.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary Booking CTA:* Vibrant Electric Violet gradient base (`linear-gradient(135deg, #8A4DFF 0%, #682AE9 100%)`) with white bold text, pill curvature, and a subtle cyan-accented tap-press feedback ring.
  - *Secondary Tactile:* Translucent surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), white text, with a crisp `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`.
- **NestAI Copilot Floating Pill:**
  - An omnipresent smart widget displaying an emoji icon, pulsing soft-purple indicator ring, and real-time textual status (e.g., "NestAI: Caregiver notified"). Tapping triggers an expansive conversational bottom sheet.
- **Chips & Filter Pills:**
  - Compact rounded capsules with `space-xs` vertical and `space-md` horizontal padding. Selected chips switch to bright Electric Violet or Neo-Mint with dark contrasting text.
- **Booking & Telehealth Cards:**
  - Layered glass container featuring doctor avatar, Neo-Mint "Available Today" badge, star ratings, and an instant 1-tap book pill. High-contrast typography ensures specialty designations are quickly scannable.
- **Form Inputs & Search:**
  - Recessed dark background (`rgba(15, 17, 26, 0.6)`) with `1px` soft lavender borders, transitioning into an electric violet glow upon keyboard activation.
- **Status & Triage Badges:**
  - High-saturation micro-pills utilizing semantic tints (Neo-Mint for stable/ready, Gentle Coral for critical prescriptions, Soft Lavender for routine consultations).
